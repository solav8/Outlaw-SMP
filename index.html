05 — FAQ

Need to know?

Anything else — prices, rewards, delivery, upgrades — just ask in Discord. That's the fastest way to get a real answer.

What comes with each rank?

Prime ($1): Prime kit, Insta RTP, Insta Home, Insta Spawn, $50,000 in-game cash, 100 points and 2 crate keys of your choice. Outlaw+ ($5): Outlaw+ kit, Insta RTP, Insta Home, Insta Spawn, $300,000 in-game cash, 1,000 points and 2 crate keys of your choice.

How do the 2 included keys work?

Every rank purchase includes two keys. Use the key picker on the rank card to choose any two crate types. If you add a rank from the hero card, it defaults to Normal + Gold. You can fine-tune both keys from the cart before checkout — including setting both dropdowns to the same crate if that's what you're feeling.

How much are crates?

Every crate key is $2 — Normal, Gold, Spawner, Seasonal, Special and Keyall. Add as many as you want to the cart; the price is per key.

How do I receive my purchase?

Add your items to the cart, hit checkout, enter your exact Minecraft username and copy the order. Then open a ticket in the Discord and paste it. Staff confirm payment and deliver your rank or keys in-game. Keep your DMs open in case they need you.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged inside your Discord ticket. This site doesn't collect card details or take payment directly. Only pay through the method staff give you in the ticket.

Can I upgrade from Prime to Outlaw+ later?

Probably — open a ticket and staff will help you sort it out. They'll confirm the upgrade price and what carries over before you pay anything.

I need more info about a crate or rank.

Join discord.gg/outlawsmp and ask. Reward lists, odds and delivery questions are all handled there.
